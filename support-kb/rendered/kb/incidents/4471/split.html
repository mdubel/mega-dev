parser: day-first dates are read month first

It covers 379 reports. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April (07/04) goes out as 4 July. It reaches every tenant, through the app, by email and through the form. This is the bug the queued fix is for. Ticket: #1.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type the month first

It covers 33 reports. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Its tenants’ people type what it says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date swaps in the form, before the parser sees it. The symptom is the same as parser, so a subject can’t tell them apart; the fix is different. Ticket: #2.

one-day: the date moves by one day

It covers 5 reports. Day and month stay in place; the date moves by a day. One meeting showed on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto, so the date crosses a time zone somewhere. The desk read these in the queue and noted them. They stay counted under parser, where they are filed, and this ticket counts them too. The parser fix won’t change them. Ticket: #3.

How this was counted

  • One report per id. The ticket system exports pages that overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • Cause from the tenant, not from intake. causeOf in causes.ts places a report by its tenant’s locale and booking form. Intake’s tag follows the reporter’s browser: it tags every en-US browser as hint, wherever the tenant is.
  • Counted where it is filed, ticketed by what it says. A report whose text shows a one-day move (desk-notes.json) stays counted under its tenant’s cause, as the desk counts, and is counted again under one-day, its own ticket, instead of a footnote.
  • A report with no date in it stays with its tenant’s cause.

Every number on this page is computed from the export when the page is built. When the desk adds reports or notes, the table follows.
